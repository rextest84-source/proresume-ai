About ProResume AI

We help job seekers create resumes that get past automated screening and into the hands of hiring managers.

Our Mission

We started ProResume AI because good candidates often lose out in applicant tracking systems before a person reads their resume. The product combines clean templates, ATS-friendly formatting, and writing suggestions you can edit before you export.

What We Do

ProResume AI is a subscription-based software platform (SaaS) that provides:

  • Writing suggestions for summaries, bullets, and skills
  • ATS-compatible professional resume templates
  • Keyword alignment suggestions from job postings
  • High-quality PDF resume export
  • Resume checklist with completeness tips

Our service is delivered entirely online through our web platform. Customers can sign up for a free plan or subscribe to Pro ($15/month) for full access to all features.
